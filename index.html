I'm Arjun B, a passionate cybersecurity enthusiast focused on uncovering and mitigating digital threats. I actively sharpen my skills on TryHackMe and Hack The Box, specialising in web exploitation, steganography, OSINT, and cryptography.

As a Member of Team Binary Misfits, I help in organising and competing in national and international CTF competitions — earning event wins. I mentor junior members and create weekly training challenges.

Also, as the Tech Team Lead for the OWASP student chapter @ Sathyabama, I host Capture The Flag (CTF) events, create challenge questions, mentor junior members, and design weekly training challenges.

I believe cybersecurity is as much about curiosity and ethics as technical skill. My goal is to build systems that are not just powerful, but fundamentally secure.
